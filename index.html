About

I'm Clarisse.

A creative by nature, and endlessly curious about the world around me.

Born in France, raised in India, and later living in England and Canada, I’ve always felt at home between cultures. Travel feeds my curiosity, while photography gives me a reason to slow down, observe and notice the details.

My work as an osteopath has shaped the care and attention I bring to people and projects. Working with clay reflects that same love of making, learning and connecting with the people behind things.

Spoken languages: English and French.

I shoot on a Fujifilm X-T2 and iPhone 18 Pro, delivering high-resolution JPEG images, with RAW files available by arrangement.

My next chapter takes me back to India and on to Southeast Asia, with my camera along for the journey.
